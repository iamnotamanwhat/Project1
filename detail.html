Featured Workshop: Hand Lettering

A beginner friendly class on turning everyday words into expressive lettering

Challenges and Solution

Most beginners think they need expensive brush pens and perfect handwriting, leading many to quit before starting. We start with simple pencil sketches, building letters and shapes step by step.

Results & Outcomes

  • Every participant leaves with one finished lettering piece
  • Learn 3 core lettering styles: serif, script and block
  • A practice sheet to keep improving at home
